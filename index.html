What your tools actually cost

The Small Operator looks at the software and services small businesses pay for: what they cost at regular prices rather than introductory ones, which conditions quietly change that cost, and what moving between them actually involves.

It is written by Jeff Peckham.

The method is deliberately plain. Vendor documentation is reported as vendor documentation. Anything tested directly is described at the scope actually tested — one account, three contacts, one email, whatever it happened to be. Anything still unknown is left marked as unknown rather than filled in with a confident guess. Where the honest answer is that you should stay where you are, or pay nobody at all, that is the answer you will get.

More on how that works, and on affiliate disclosure, is on the about and editorial policy page.
